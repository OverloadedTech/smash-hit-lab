## 2026-09-10: Smash Hit room rotation semantics

Does `mgSetRotation(-1)` rotate room geometry, camera orientation or physics, and what controls its pace? I followed the registered current x86_64 Lua binding at ELF 0x283070, resolved its PLT calls to `QiScriptArgs::asInt` and `asFloat`, inspected `Room::getProgress`, `Level::update`, `Display::update`, `Level::getGravity` and their callers, then compared the ARM64 decompilation and decoded the native float constants.

The binding stores an integer-derived turn count at Room+0x47e4 and a float amplitude at +0x47e8, and an omitted amplitude is zero. Room progress is `(-playerZ-roomOffset)/roomLength`, eased over the first and last 10 percent. The roll angle is `2*pi*turns*easedProgress`, or the sine of that phase multiplied by a nonzero amplitude. The resulting Z-axis quaternion goes to Level+0x32c and +0x128 and then the display camera. Gravity is the same rotation applied to the base `(0,-10*roomGravity,0)` vector. Verified native calls from `Physics::update` at 0x289449 and `Debris::update` at 0x279211 consume `Level::getGravity`. The VR cave comments out its call, while Ocean, Hyperspace and other retained VR scripts still call the API.

Phone 1.5.14 camera roll based on traveled room fraction, with a corresponding physical gravity rotation, is CONFIRMED, HIGH confidence for the inspected phone binding, instructions, constants and call paths. This API does not collectively rotate all static room geometry, the optional second argument is sine amplitude in radians rather than a duration, and VR-wide native behavior remains untested.
